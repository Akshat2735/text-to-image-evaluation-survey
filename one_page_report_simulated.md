# One-Page Report: Text-to-Image Evaluation for Indian Product Imagery

> **SIMULATED EXAMPLE DATA:** The 15 participants and all numerical findings below are hypothetical. They are provided as a presentation draft only, not as collected study results.

## Eval

This project evaluates text-to-image outputs for Indian e-commerce product photography. The categories are copper water bottles, Kanjeevaram silk sarees, Kolhapuri leather chappals, and stainless-steel Indian tiffin boxes. These categories test both practical catalog quality and cultural/product-specific accuracy.

## Core setup

Hypothetical participants saw four anonymized images per prompt, labeled A, B, C, and D. For every image they rated Relevance, Visual Quality, and India-fit on a 1-5 scale. They also selected one overall favorite per category. The survey summarizes favorite counts and average ratings overall and by prompt group.

Category leaders in this simulated example:

C is the simulated overall leader, especially for Saree and Tiffin. A leads the Shoes category, suggesting that the preferred output can change with the product rather than remaining constant across all prompts.

## Most important takeaway

For AI systems serving India, image quality should not be measured by visual polish alone. A useful evaluation should separately measure prompt relevance, professional visual quality, and India-fit across multiple local product categories. This makes category-specific strengths and cultural inaccuracies easier to identify.

## Next scale

A real follow-up should recruit at least 30-50 diverse participants, expand the product set, randomize image order and letter assignments, and report rating distributions, confidence intervals, inter-rater agreement, and qualitative error tags. The simulated numbers above must be replaced with actual responses before the report is used as a factual submission.
